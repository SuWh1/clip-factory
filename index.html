Clip Factory

A private tool that finds the best moments in long gaming videos and streams and turns them into YouTube Shorts for its owner's own channels.

What it does

  • Finds moments worth clipping in long videos and streams.
  • Cuts them into vertical Shorts with captions.
  • Sends each clip to its owner for approval before anything is posted.
  • Uploads approved clips to the owner's YouTube channels and reads their view counts.

Who it is for

Clip Factory runs on one computer and is used only by its owner for channels they own or manage. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
